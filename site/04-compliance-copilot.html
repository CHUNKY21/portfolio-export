Compliance Co-Pilot

A concept where a research agent, a policy-check agent, and an escalation agent handle parts of submission review — with every action visible on a timestamped trail.

"I never know why my submission is stuck, or who's actually looking at it."

Introduction

What's Compliance Co-Pilot?

A concept extending Michael's real Credit Suisse Outside Business Interests (OBI) work: three specialized agents handle context-gathering, policy-conflict flagging, and escalation routing, instead of purely manual step-by-step review.

Target audience
Risk & Compliance manager

The problems

  1. Submissions returned for missing information, with no clear explanation
  2. No visibility into where a submission is in the review pipeline or why
  3. No consolidated view of a submitter's disclosure history for judgment calls

Extends design and discovery work from Michael's real Credit Suisse OBI case study into this forward-looking multi-agent concept.

Design / Process

Discovery & Research — real interviews carried over from OBI. New: mapped which parts of the workflow are agent-automatable vs. requiring human compliance judgment.

Design Thinking & Ideation — centered on agent behavior: what each agent surfaces, how conflicting outputs are shown, where agent-proposes/human-confirms sits.

Design Sprints & Iteration / Usability Testing — not yet run; this is a concept/vision piece, no fabricated test results are presented.

Unexpected challenge

The hardest open question isn't agent accuracy — it's whether reviewers will trust a workflow where three different agents can each touch one case.

Results (targets, not measured)

~30%
faster review cycle (target)
~50%
fewer missing-info returns (target)
≥80%
reviewer trust target

Key Insight

"When more than one agent can act on a case, the UX problem shifts from 'is the AI right' to 'can the user tell who did what and why.' The activity trail isn't a feature bolted onto the workflow — it's the design's answer to that question."
