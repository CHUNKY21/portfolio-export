Introduction

What's the Meridian Research Agent?

A conversational agent embedded in Meridian, Anchorpoint Capital's investment operations platform, that retrieves, synthesizes, and cites answers to plain-language research questions.

Target audience
Investment operations analyst

The problems

  1. Manually opening 4-5 systems for a single research question
  2. No consistent starting point — every analyst improvises their own process
  3. No way to show your work after the fact — answers aren't auditable
  4. Repetitive, low-judgment lookups take the same time as genuinely hard investigations

Led the design process for this concept, from discovery interviews through the shipped v1 experience.

Design / Process

Discovery & Research — real interviews with analysts and their team lead. New in AI-native version: mapped what data sources could plausibly answer with confidence vs. requiring human judgment.

Design Thinking & Ideation — centered on agent behavior before any screen: what happens when it's uncertain, when a user corrects it, where the hand-off line sits.

Design Sprints & Iteration — real, iterative sprints with weekly usability testing.

Usability Testing — tested the happy path and failure path as equally important; watched whether analysts verified sources unprompted.

What we learned

Early versions surfaced confidence only when the agent flagged low confidence itself. Testing showed analysts trusted the agent more when confidence was shown on every answer.
