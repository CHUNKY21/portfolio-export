Compliance Alert Triage Copilot
Redesigning alert triage for a wealth management compliance team
The Problem

Compliance officers received 200–350 daily alerts with 65% false positives, spending 6+ hours a day on manual triage.

Our Solution

An adaptive triage dashboard where every alert arrives pre-explained — risk score, evidence, and a plain-language rationale.

The Application Concept Has a 3 Step Process
1RESEARCH SYNTHESIS

Claude analyzes 12 months of alert logs and officer resolution notes to extract recurring decision patterns.

2CONFIDENCE SCORING

Each alert is scored with supporting evidence and a natural-language explanation of why it was flagged.

3FEEDBACK LOOP

Officer overrides retrain the scoring logic, so the system improves with real-world use.
